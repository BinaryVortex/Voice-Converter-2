# Voice Converter 2

A simple web-based voice converter application that allows you to convert text to speech and speech to text using your browser's built-in APIs.

## Features

- **Text to Speech**: Enter text and convert it to spoken audio using various voice options.
- **Speech to Text**: Speak into your microphone and convert your voice to text.
- **Multiple Voices**: Choose from available speech synthesis voices on your device.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Clean and intuitive UI for easy navigation.
- **Browser-Based**: No installation required, works directly in modern web browsers.

## Usage

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
3. Choose between "Text to Voice" or "Voice to Text" conversion.
4. For Text to Voice:
   - Enter your text in the textarea.
   - Select a voice from the dropdown.
   - Click "Convert To Voice" to hear the audio.
5. For Voice to Text:
   - Click "Start Speaking" and speak into your microphone.
   - The transcribed text will appear in the textarea.

## Browser Support

- Chrome: Full support
- Firefox: Full support
- Safari: Full support (may require user gesture for speech recognition)
- Edge: Full support

Note: Speech recognition requires microphone access and may not work in all browsers or environments.
